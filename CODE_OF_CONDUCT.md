# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The BOSC Community Library community is committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, collaborative, and harassment-free environment for all contributors regardless of background, experience level, nationality, gender, or technical expertise.

We aim to promote professionalism, open learning, transparency, and positive community engagement throughout the project.

---

## Expected Behavior

All contributors are expected to:

- Use respectful, welcoming, and professional language
- Respect differing viewpoints and technical opinions
- Accept constructive criticism positively
- Support new contributors and encourage open learning
- Focus on collaboration, innovation, and community growth
- Follow project contribution guidelines and ethical OSS practices
- Maintain honesty and originality in all contributions

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are prohibited within the BOSC Community Library community:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Personal attacks or abusive communication
- Trolling, insulting comments, or hostile behavior
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission
- Plagiarism or intentionally harmful contributions
- Disruptive conduct that negatively affects collaboration

---

## Reporting Issues

Community members are encouraged to report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through repository issue discussions or other appropriate communication channels.

All reports will be reviewed respectfully and handled professionally.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove, reject, edit, or restrict contributions that violate these standards.

Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from participation in the project community.

---

## Conclusion

The BOSC Community Library promotes an open-source culture built on respect, professionalism, transparency, collaboration, and shared learning for all contributors.
